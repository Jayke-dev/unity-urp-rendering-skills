## Material selection is now an actionable lesson

The first-render failure was narrowed beyond pass-count changes: effective material tags followed an observed active SubShader transition from 2 to 0. A frozen effective-tag comparison mistook that selection change for mutation. A fresh-material diagnostic, independently checked against indexed shader declarations, supported a selection-aware tag comparison while retaining the other material checks.

The corrected native stages passed **33 EditMode + 20 PlayMode + 7 regression + 1 readiness = 61 checks**, with zero failures/skips and known successful process exits. The original first-render case passed while reproducing the same selection transition. This establishes the tested material/visibility path, not final terrain rendering. Read the [reusable guard reference](../skills/unity-urp-render-debugging/references/selection-aware-material-guards.md) before applying the pattern elsewhere.

## The first real V3 bake passed

One actual offline bake completed with **25 bake gates**, two internal determinism passes and nine independently reviewed binding gates. Output geometry was **16,014 vertices / 28,385 triangles**, within the declared **16,425 / 31,104** limits. The reported composite water oracle checked **665,280 segments with zero mismatches**. The heightfield, exact copied base and PNG texture integrity were independently checked.

Those counts are properties of this candidate and its reviewed domain, not portable design constants. Determinism proves repeatable output for the tested inputs; it does not prove correct import, pixel preservation or visual quality. Inspecting the generated texture atlas is also not inspecting the landscape through the real camera.

## Bake bytes, imported resources and final images are separate stages

The follow-on task pinned three reviewed asset hashes but stopped before its imported-resource contract test and documentation were complete. Nothing in that partial round established successful Unity import, the strict two-view pilot, the final 16-view comparison or headset performance. Preserve partial files as unreviewed work, not as a completed source release.

A reusable handoff should record the last source/native/bake identities, what completed, which artifacts remain unreviewed and the exact next validation. Do not restart an expensive bake merely because the following task was interrupted. Verify the stored source/input/output bindings first. Resume only within the project's actual model and execution authorisation.
