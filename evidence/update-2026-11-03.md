# 3 November update: route coverage, clone causality and diagnostic validity

Evidence cutoff: **2026-11-03 13:08 UTC**, through the completed R121 draft review. Ongoing R122 work is excluded. The [1 November snapshot](update-2026-11-01.md) remains historical. New aggregate excerpts and source-report hashes are in [update-2026-11-03.json](update-2026-11-03.json).

**Reference limit:** these native passes used an explicitly unaccepted reconstructed entrance reference. They do not establish equality with the original missing serialized files. No final full-16-view, artistic or target-headset acceptance is claimed.

## What improved in actual Unity runs?

- Eight entrance checks and both real terrain resource-import checks passed. The asset-import stage that had been unfinished at the quota stop was completed on this reconstructed candidate.
- A route-dependent basin changed from inactive to drawing between courtyard and approach. Stronger readiness included it in relevant water coverage (28→29), and a fresh verified bake retained identical geometry/texture bytes with updated provenance.
- The next two-view run showed the terrain in both views, with zero water-mask/colour, Entrance, gap and hole differences in those two views. Foreground preservation still failed at **49 and 70 pixels**, and installed-OFF still differed by **2 pixels**. The visual review also found the landforms too subdued for the artistic brief.

## Which lessons are worth reusing?

1. **Route state belongs in visibility proofs.** An object disabled at bind may become relevant later. Diagnose the actual guard category rather than assuming the old sky issue recurred.
2. **Counterfactual clones need final-state fidelity.** Comparing flags before a bounds override produced an apparent mismatch; post-override field comparisons resolved it. Matching meshes/materials alone is insufficient.
3. **Do not subtract clone totals to claim causation.** Retaining the original changes depth/occlusion. Residual-set overlap narrows a path but does not identify a unique writer. In one view, all 29 sector residuals overlapped the real 70 with identical RGB, leaving 41 real-only.
4. **A plausible queue change can be worse.** Source-queue clone controls increased sector residuals from 4→11 (13 repeat) and 29→66. The tested change was rejected; neither queue becomes a universal setting.
5. **Pose equality is not image-state equality.** An active comfort vignette invalidated a purported post-teardown persistence control. Extra diagnostic renders also changed the sequence, so not reproducing a large old failure did not erase it.
6. **Replay colour is not pixel history.** Draft review found unsupported target mapping, last-change attribution and missing positive controls. A null-associated batch was misclassified as non-draw. These were static/compiled counterexamples, not newly observed Unity Frame Debugger behaviour.
7. **Tool cleanup must handle partial side effects.** A reproduced draft path enabled a debugger-like state, threw before recording ownership and skipped shutdown. Exception and iterator-disposal paths need their own meaningful tests.
