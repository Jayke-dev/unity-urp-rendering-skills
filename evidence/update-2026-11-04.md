# 4 November update: preservation-first design and a no-install control

Evidence cutoff: **2026-11-04 05:13 UTC**. This addendum covers completed native receipts through R135, R137 source review and the R138 **worker source report only**. Subsequent supervisor review and overlay rendering are excluded. [Aggregate evidence and report hashes](update-2026-11-04.json) continue E21–E26; earlier snapshots remain historical.

The environment remains Unity 6000.3.26f1 / URP 17.3.0 / desktop Metal. Native tests use an **unaccepted reconstructed reference**. No complete landscape, historical-baseline equality, full-view acceptance or Quest result is claimed.

## A different architecture achieved bounded preservation

The earlier replacement retired the existing mountains and introduced copied base surfaces plus terrain. A same-frame diagnostic hid both owned groups while the original stayed retired. None of the known 70 pixels recovered: 41 retained the candidate colour and 29 changed to other colours. Exact before/after controls and cleanup passed; the result remained mixed. It did not prove a writer or retirement cause (E21).

The next prototype preserved the original mountains drawing and added geometrically separated distant terrain. Its own two-view captures reported **zero foreground residuals, water-mask/colour differences, Entrance changes, gaps and holes**. Live geometry binding and lifecycle checks passed. The separate first-frame-to-OFF difference remained two pixels and the overall capture test still failed (E22).

This establishes a usable preservation direction under those conditions, not the artistic result. Image review rejected the look: the original flat tan faces dominated and most new terrain was hidden. Do not portray 49/70 → 0/0 as a same-candidate causal improvement curve across processes or references.

## The two-pixel observation predates installation in a controlled sequence

A follow-up added a never-installed repeat before installation, within the same frame:

| Comparison | Changed pixels |
|---|---:|
| First image → never-installed repeat | 2 |
| Never-installed repeat → installed but OFF | 0 |

The two differences were one 8-bit channel step at most. Independent comparison of saved PNGs agreed. Both ON views still had zero foreground residuals and unchanged water/Entrance checks. The original first-frame assertion **still failed** and was retained (E23).

This supports capture first-render/repeat behaviour for this observation. It does not establish a unique engine cause or authorize discarding the first frame, subtracting noise or changing acceptance. An alternative interpretation was drafted separately and remained unapplied at cutoff.

## Raising terrain was tested and rejected

A candidate lifted 2,365 samples by an average 55.5 m, within the existing 180 m height cap. Geometry and lifecycle checks passed. The new far band became more visible but still looked broad and plain; one foreground pixel failed at spawn, while the other view, water and Entrance stayed exact. The candidate was rejected and height tuning stopped (E24).

The useful design lesson is to distinguish visibility, silhouette, surface detail and depth. More visible triangles cannot supply missing rock, grass or erosion detail on the mountain faces that still dominate the image.

## What the surface-overlay work does and does not establish

Source review corrected an assumption that a baked foot-strip selection represented the whole mountain surface: that selection contains 636 triangles; the verified full drawn slice contains 2,862. A subset resource is not a full-surface proof. A live full-slice route can use the existing verifier, but hashes and exact positions alone do not prove raster coverage.

R137 also corrected a comparison draft to validate all three complete frames before pixel access; missing or short evidence becomes INVALID. Nine independent pure checks and a stub-assisted compile passed. Neither the original acceptance contract nor a product shader changed (E25).

R138 delivered a temporary coverage-smoke helper and constant-key overlay shader. Its report lists source/pure checks, but **no Unity shader import, full project compilation or render**. Supervisor review was pending at this cutoff (E26). Do not reuse it as a validated coating implementation.

Even a future two-view resolved-RGB match would not prove per-sample MSAA identity, arbitrary-view or stereo coverage, no co-depth leaks, unchanged reflections/refraction, artistic quality or headset performance. Those remain separate observations and decisions.
